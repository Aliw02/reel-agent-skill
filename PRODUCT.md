# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A solo video creator working locally on one reel at a time. The creator needs to review pacing, bilingual captions, motion design, audio, and the final render without losing control of the edit.

## Product Purpose

AI Reel Studio turns raw talking-head footage into a polished vertical reel through a human-controlled four-stage workflow. The creator can ask the director copilot for changes, see proposed plan updates and the Remotion result live, and apply only the changes they approve.

## Brand Personality

Focused, cinematic, precise. The interface should feel like a quiet director console rather than a noisy effects panel.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboard layouts, decorative glass panels without purpose, dense control clutter, noisy animation, and automatic AI changes that cannot be reviewed or reverted.

## Design Principles

- Keep the creator in control of every production gate.
- Show the visual consequence of an edit before committing it.
- Favor clear editorial decisions over more effects.
- Keep the workspace calm while making important changes visible.
- Preserve a reversible canonical edit plan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Provide complete keyboard access, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, descriptive labels, screen-reader status updates for streamed work, and reduced-motion behavior for live previews and transitions.
